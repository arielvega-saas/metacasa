```
Home Finance is the personal finance app built for the whole household. Track your spending, build a budget that actually sticks, and reach your savings goals — on your own or together with your family, all in one place.

Unlike other budgeting apps, Home Finance is designed for households: invite your partner or family and everyone sees the same budget, the same accounts and the same goals, each from their own phone. No spreadsheets, no chaos.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BUDGETING THAT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Envelope budgeting: set a limit for each category and see what's left in real time.
• Zero-based budgeting: give every dollar a job, the way the best budgeting systems do.
• Alerts when you're about to overspend (80%, 95%, 100%).
• Waterfall strategy: set what percentage flows to savings and investments automatically.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FULL CONTROL OF YOUR SPENDING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Log income and expenses in seconds, with categories and subcategories.
• Recurring transactions for paychecks, rent and bills.
• Filters, search and a spending calendar so you know where your money goes.
• Spending heatmap plus monthly and annual reports.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ACCOUNTS, CARDS & NET WORTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Add bank accounts, cash, savings, investments and credit cards.
• Track your net worth (assets minus debts) at a glance.
• Credit card management: limit, statement date and due date.
• Debt payoff plan with a projection of when you'll be debt-free.
• Installment purchases with payment-by-payment tracking.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GOALS & BILLS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Create savings goals (vacation, emergency fund, anything) and see the estimated date you'll hit them.
• Bill reminders so you never pay a late fee again.
• Smart notifications for goals, recurring expenses and envelopes about to run out.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AI ASSISTANT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Log expenses by voice, no typing.
• Scan a receipt with your camera and let the AI enter it for you.
• The assistant only turns on with your explicit consent. Your passwords and card numbers are NEVER sent.
```
